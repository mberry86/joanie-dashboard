objection handling — 8 response cards
"We already have call sources."
Our calls are consumer-initiated at the exact moment of shopping — not recycled or re-marketed. Our 700 QM agents validate quality on your product mix before it ever reaches you. Run us head-to-head on conversion for 30 days — your data will make the case.
"Why is Centerfield selling calls? Aren't you a competitor?"
We operate QM as our own agency — we use these calls first. When our agents aren't available, calls that would go unbid become marketplace inventory. We're not competing for your enrollments — we're solving a capacity gap while giving you premium media we already buy for our own operation.
"We're worried about TCPA compliance."
Full TCPA consent chain on every call. Recordings available for audit. AHIP-certified QM agents. We cleared compliance with Centene and AmeriLife — same documentation package, one 30-min meeting with your compliance team.
"We need to test before AEP commitment."
That's exactly our 500-call pilot — no commitment beyond the trial, 30 days, fixed CPL. At day 30 we review your conversion data together. If it doesn't outperform your current sources, we don't ask for the AEP contract. Risk is on us.
"Your CPL is higher than what we pay now."
Higher CPL on lower-intent calls is a worse deal. Run the math on cost-per-enrollment: if our conversion is 2× your current source, we're less expensive per enrollment even at a 40% CPL premium. Happy to model that with your actual disposition data.
"Too many priorities before AEP."
A 500-call pilot takes 2 weeks to stand up. If you wait until August, you won't have the conversion data to commit AEP volume before Oct 1 — and you'll miss the highest-CPL period of the year. Start the pilot now, decide with data in July.
"We only work with exclusive call sources."
Our marketplace calls are live transfers routed exclusively to one buyer at a time — not shared leads. Consumer-initiated, state-matched, product-filtered, going directly to your agent team. Not simultaneously available to other buyers.
"We're not licensed in all those states."
We route exclusively to your licensed state footprint — you only receive calls from states where your agents can sell. The 111K Ohio missed calls only go to a buyer licensed in Ohio. State matching is built into our routing, not your problem to manage.
