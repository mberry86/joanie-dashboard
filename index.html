objection handling
"We already have call sources."
We're not replacing your sources — we're supplementing with incremental AEP volume your agents aren't getting today. Can your current sources scale 3–4× for Q4? Ours can. Want to run a 500-call pilot to compare quality side-by-side?
"Your calls are more expensive."
Our CPL reflects intent quality — these are in-funnel consumers, not recycled leads. The conversion rate difference pays for itself. We can model your current CPA against ours. What's your current cost per enrollment?
"We're happy with what we have."
Then this is about adding a high-quality second source for AEP, not replacing anything. When your primary source can't keep pace with your 700+ agent capacity in October, you need a backup with volume. That's exactly what we offer.
"How are you different from [Competitor X]?"
The key difference is the 700-agent QM operation. We don't just source and route calls — we run 700 licensed agents who validate every call type before it reaches you. That quality layer is unique to Centerfield.
"We need to check compliance."
We'll walk your compliance team through our TCPA documentation, consent chain, and call recording in one 30-minute meeting. We cleared compliance with Centene and AmeriLife — same package. Let's get that on the calendar.
"The timing isn't right."
AEP is the right time — it's when your agents have the most capacity to take high-intent calls. The onboarding window closes end of Q2. Compliance + routing + testing take 4–6 weeks. Start now or miss the AEP ramp.
the pilot close
The offer: "Let us run a 500-call paid pilot at a preferred rate. You get full call recording, disposition data, and conversion reporting — no long-term commitment. If the quality is there, we talk AEP volume. If not, you've spent almost nothing. This is exactly how we started with Centene and AmeriLife."
